Game Flow
- game will have 10 rounds
- on each ropund participants will se an image and the same question always "It is A or B?"
- participants must answer the question based on the image they see.
- partiicpants will have 10 seconds to answer on each round
- after all participants answered or time finished, the correct answer will be revealed with a new image
- after the answer reveal, 5 seconds later will start a new round.
- at the end of the 10 rounds, a final leaderboard will be shown with the winner
